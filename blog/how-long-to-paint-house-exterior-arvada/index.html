How long does it take to paint a house exterior in Arvada?

Days on site, weeks on the calendar, and the Arvada-specific things that stretch both.

Get a free estimateCall 720-912-4676

Updated October 25, 2026 · By Bighorn Painting, Arvada

Short answer: a typical Arvada exterior takes about three to six working days on site: roughly a day to wash and dry, one or two for scraping, repairs and caulk, and two or three for primer and finish coats. Hardboard repair, a second story, HOA approval, lead-safe rules on older homes and the weather can each add days on site or weeks on the calendar.

Days on site versus weeks on the calendar

Most answers online describe an average house with a crew of three and land between about three days and a week. That matches what you should expect for the painting itself. What those answers leave out is everything that happens before the crew arrives, and in Arvada that is often the longer part.

Why Arvada exteriors run longer than the national answer

The houses are older than they look

Census estimates for Arvada (ACS 2024, 5-year) show 52.5% of the city’s roughly 52,000 homes were built before 1980, and 41.6% went up in the 1960s and 1970s alone. Houses from that era have more paint layers, more trim, and often original single-pane window frames that need glazing or caulk before anyone opens a can.

Hardboard repair is carpentry first

A lot of Arvada homes from the late 1960s through the 1980s wear hardboard siding. Swollen boards at the bottom course have to be cut out, replaced and sealed before they are painted, and that work runs at carpentry speed, not painting speed. A dozen failed sections can add a full day or more. Our guide to repairing hardboard siding before painting explains how to spot it on your own house.

Two stories and walkout lots need staging

Ladders and planks have to be moved and reset for every section above first-floor height. On a split-level or a walkout basement lot, the downhill side can be close to three stories, and that side alone can take as long as the rest of the house.

The west wall needs more work

South and west elevations take the most sun and usually carry the most peeling, so they eat a disproportionate share of the prep time. If you are curious why, read why your west wall fails first.

The weather window is shorter than it looks

NOAA’s 1991–2020 normals for Denver’s airport put the median last spring freeze around May 5 and the median first fall freeze around October 9. The National Weather Service’s longer Denver record gives May 5 and October 7. Those are 50-50 dates, not cutoffs, but they frame the season.

Paint labels set the real limits. Sherwin-Williams Duration Exterior, for example, can go on as low as 35°F, but not if the air or surface may drop below 35°F within 48 hours, and between 35°F and 45°F it needs 24 to 48 hours before a second coat instead of about 4. In practice, a job that takes four days in June can take six in late September because the second coat waits on the afternoon warmth.

If you are reading this in late October, most exterior work in Arvada is running day by day on the forecast. It is a good time to get an estimate, sort out HOA approval over the winter, and book a spring start, while interiors fill the cold months.

Lead-safe rules on older homes

For homes built before 1978, federal rules require renovation work that disturbs more than about 20 square feet of exterior paint to be done by an EPA-certified firm using lead-safe practices: plastic on the ground, wet scraping, no open-flame burning or high-speed sanding without containment, and a verified cleanup. Colorado adds the 7-day pamphlet notice described above. None of this has to add weeks, but it does add hours on every side of the house, and a bid that ignores it on a 1965 house is a bid to be skeptical of.

How to keep your project on schedule

  1. Pick colors early. Test samples on the south and north walls, because the same chip looks different in each light.
  2. Start the HOA submission as soon as you have colors and a written scope.
  3. Turn off the sprinklers a day or two before work starts and keep them off until the paint is cured.
  4. Trim shrubs back from the siding and move grills, planters and patio furniture.
  5. Tell us about the bad spots. Photos of peeling, soft boards or rot help us plan the right number of days instead of discovering them mid-job.
  6. Choose your estimate style. In person or from photos, both are free. Older homes with suspected rot usually go better in person.

Questions

Common questions

How many days does it take to paint the outside of a 2,000 square foot house?

Published estimates for an average house with a crew of three run from about three days to a week of on-site work. A 2,000 square foot Arvada ranch with sound paint sits near the short end. The same house as a two-story, or with a dozen failed hardboard boards along the bottom course, can run a week or more. Your written estimate should give a number of working days, not just a start date.

Can you paint a house exterior in late October or November in Arvada?

Sometimes, on a day-by-day basis. Some exterior paints can go on as low as 35°F, but the label also requires that air and surface stay above that for the next 48 hours, and coats take much longer to dry in the cold. NOAA puts the median first fall freeze at Denver’s airport around October 9, so after mid-October exterior work runs day by day on the forecast, and interiors fill the gaps.

Do I need to be home while my exterior is being painted?

No. Most homeowners are at work for most of it. We do need access to outdoor outlets and water, gates unlocked, pets inside, and cars moved away from the house on spray or scraping days. It helps to be reachable by phone, and to be home for the final walkthrough so you can point at anything you want touched up.

How long after painting can it rain?

It depends on the product and the temperature, so the label is the authority. As one example, Sherwin-Williams Duration Exterior asks for no rain or snow within 2 to 3 hours of application, and allows a recoat after about 4 hours at 45°F and above but 24 to 48 hours between 35°F and 45°F. Light rain on a fresh coat usually means a touch-up, not a redo.

How long does HOA paint approval take in Arvada?

Plan on weeks, not days. Many Arvada HOAs, Candelas among them, review color changes at a scheduled board or committee meeting, so the timing depends on when you submit. Repainting in the exact existing colors is sometimes handled faster, but check your covenants. Our Candelas painting page covers what the submission usually needs.

Does lead paint make an exterior job take longer?

Yes, a little. On homes built before 1978, Colorado requires the contractor to hand you the EPA Renovate Right pamphlet at least 7 days before work that disturbs 2 square feet or more of painted surface. Federal lead-safe rules then add plastic sheeting on the ground, wet scraping and a careful cleanup, which usually adds hours per side rather than whole days. Census data shows over half of Arvada homes were built before 1980.
