Why Arvada homes are different

Hardboard siding is everywhere here

If your home went up between 1968 and 1990, the siding is probably hardboard, sometimes called masonite. It swells and delaminates where water reaches an unsealed edge, usually at the bottom course or under a window. Painting over a swollen board accomplishes nothing and traps the moisture inside.

  • We check every elevation and count the failed sections at the estimate
  • Failed board is cut out and replaced, not coated over
  • Every cut end gets sealed, including the ones the original crew left bare
  • You get the repair count in writing, not the words repair as needed

Textured walls, inside almost every house

Knockdown, orange peel and skip trowel are the standard on Arvada's interior walls. A patch that is flat where the wall is textured stays visible under every lamp in the room, which is why texture matching is part of our interior scope rather than an upgrade.

  • We identify and match the texture you actually have
  • Repairs are textured, primed and painted so they disappear
  • Pre-1980 popcorn ceilings get tested before anyone disturbs them
  • Smooth skim-coat conversions quoted honestly, mess included

West-side sun and foothills wind

Arvada sits on the open northwest edge of the metro. West and south elevations take hours of direct afternoon sun, and downslope winds come off the foothills carrying grit. Those walls commonly fail a full cycle before the north and east sides do.

  • Elevations sequenced by time of day rather than convenience
  • Higher-grade product specified where the load is heaviest
  • Phasing by elevation offered when budget is tight
  • North-wall mildew treated chemically, not painted over

Arvada painting questions

Who are the best painters in Arvada, CO?

Compare documents rather than claims. Ask every bidder for a certificate of insurance, a written scope naming each preparation step, the exact product line and sheen, the coat count, and the warranty in writing. Bighorn Painting provides all five, is based in Arvada, and is rated 4.9 stars from 95 Google reviews.

How much does it cost to paint a house in Arvada?

Published 2026 Front Range data puts exterior house painting at roughly $1.55 to $4.10 per square foot of paintable surface and interior painting at about $1.50 to $3.50 per square foot. On Arvada homes, hardboard siding repair is the factor most likely to move the number.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, always, in person or from photos. There is no charge and no pressure afterward.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, insured, with the certificate provided at the estimate. Colorado does not issue a statewide painting license, so insurance and a written scope are the documents that actually protect you.

What paint brands do you use?

Sherwin-Williams and PPG. The specific line and sheen are named per surface in your written estimate so you can read the manufacturer's data sheet yourself.

When can you paint my exterior?

Generally mid-May through early October. Denver-area records put the average last spring freeze near May 5 and the first fall freeze near October 7. The overnight low governs, because coatings cure through the night.

What does the 5-year warranty cover?

Our workmanship: the surface preparation and the application. If our work fails within five years we return and correct it. The coating also carries its own manufacturer warranty.

Do you work in Lakewood, Wheat Ridge or Golden?

Yes. Those cities have their own Bighorn Painting sites with local detail, and the same crews do the work.
