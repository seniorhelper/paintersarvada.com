Twenty-two questions, answered plainly

Hiring a painter

Who are the best painters in Arvada, CO?

Compare documents instead of adjectives. Ask every bidder for a certificate of insurance, a written scope naming each preparation step, the exact product line and sheen per surface, the coat count, and the warranty in writing. A bid that cannot produce all five is cheaper for a reason.

Does Colorado license painting contractors?

There is no statewide painting license in Colorado. Some municipalities have registration requirements. What protects you is current liability insurance, a detailed written scope and a warranty with terms you can read.

How many bids should I get?

Two or three, and ask all of them the same written questions. More than that mostly produces numbers that are not comparable, because the scopes underneath them differ.

Why is one bid so much lower?

On an Arvada exterior it is usually hardboard repair being left out. Inside, it is usually texture matching. Both are real work, both are invisible in a one-page quote, and both are why one number is thousands below another.

Scheduling and seasons

When can you paint my exterior?

Generally mid-May through early October. Denver-area records put the average last spring freeze near May 5 and the first fall freeze near October 7. The overnight low governs, because coatings have to cure through the night.

Can you paint inside during winter?

Yes, and it is the better time. Interior work happens inside a heated house, so weather is irrelevant and the calendar between November and March is far more open.

How far ahead should I book?

For exteriors, as early as possible. Everyone in Jefferson County is working inside the same twenty weeks. Interiors can usually be scheduled within a couple of weeks off-season.

What happens if it rains?

We stop, protect the work and resume when conditions allow. Weather delays are part of painting in Colorado, and we build realistic contingency in rather than promising dates the sky controls.

The work itself

What preparation is included?

Protection of everything not being painted, washing, scraping to a sound edge, sanding transitions, filling and caulking, and priming bare or repaired substrate. Each step is itemized by surface in your estimate.

How many coats do you apply?

Two finish coats as standard, with primer specified separately wherever the substrate needs it. The count is written into the estimate rather than left to interpretation.

Which paint brands do you use?

Sherwin-Williams and PPG. The exact line and sheen are named per surface so you can look up the manufacturer's own data.

Do you move furniture?

We move and protect larger pieces. Clearing small items, electronics and artwork beforehand speeds things up and can reduce the cost.

Will the same crew be here each day?

Yes, and you will have the schedule in advance. If weather moves a day, you hear it from us rather than finding an empty driveway.

Arvada specifics

What is hardboard siding and do I have it?

Hardboard, often called masonite, is compressed wood fiber used widely on Arvada homes built from the late 1960s through about 1990. The tell is swollen, soft bottom edges with paint cracking along the swell. Press it: sound board is firm, failing board gives.

Can you match my wall texture?

Yes. Knockdown, orange peel and skip trowel are all matched routinely, which matters because nearly every Arvada interior is textured. An unmatched patch stays visible permanently.

My home was built in 1962. What changes?

Lead-safe work practices. The federal EPA Renovation, Repair and Painting rule requires certified firms and specific containment and cleanup when disturbing painted surfaces in pre-1978 housing.

Do I need HOA approval?

In the newer west-side communities, usually yes, including Candelas, Leyden Rock and Five Parks. We prepare the samples and submission materials, though the board's schedule is the board's.

Why does my west wall look worse than the rest?

Direct afternoon sun. Ultraviolet breaks down the binder holding pigment, producing fading and then chalking. Arvada's open west-side position makes that load heavier here than further into the metro.

Money

How much will my project cost?

Published 2026 Front Range data puts exteriors at roughly $1.55 to $4.10 per square foot and interiors at about $1.50 to $3.50. Your number depends on condition, siding repair, texture work and access. The estimate is measured on your actual property.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Free in person or from photos.

When do I pay?

A deposit reserves your dates, and the balance is due at completion after your walkthrough. Terms are on the estimate.

Can I phase the work to spread cost?

Yes, and on Arvada exteriors it is often sensible. West and south elevations typically need attention a cycle before north and east.
