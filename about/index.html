The difference shows up in small ways that matter: we know which streets in Lake Arbor have the hardboard problem, we know Candelas boards meet monthly, and we know what the wind does on the west side in April.

How we work

  • Preparation is the job. Anyone can apply color. Whether it lasts eight years or three is decided before the finish coat opens.
  • Bad news early. If your siding needs repair before paint, you hear it at the estimate. That makes our number higher than someone who stays quiet, and we would rather lose the bid than take money for work that will fail.
  • Write everything down. Surfaces, preparation by surface, product line, sheen, coat count, schedule, terms, warranty. If it matters, it is on the page.
  • Do it once. The cheapest paint job is the one nobody repeats.

What a project looks like

  1. You call or send photos. Tell us what is bothering you and the year the house was built.
  2. We scope it. Photos are often enough. Peeling, suspected water damage or a commercial scope gets a walkthrough.
  3. Written estimate. Everything named, including any siding or drywall repair counted individually.
  4. Colors and approvals. Samples on your actual walls, and any HOA submission prepared before we schedule.
  5. Protect and prepare. The stage that decides the outcome.
  6. Coat it. Inside the manufacturer's conditions, sequenced around sun and wind, cleaned up daily.
  7. You walk it. We fix anything you flag before we ask for the balance. Then five years of written coverage.

Who we work for

Homeowners across every Arvada neighborhood, landlords and property managers with turnover work, HOA boards running phased community cycles, and small businesses along Ralston Road, Wadsworth and in Olde Town.
